10 Tips to Improve Your Video Editing Skills

Alex Johnson Video Editor December 15, 2026 5 min read 12 Comments

Video editing is both an art and a science. Whether you're a beginner just starting out or an experienced editor looking to refine your craft, there's always room for improvement. In this article, I'll share 10 practical tips that have helped me elevate my editing skills over the years.

1. Master Your Keyboard Shortcuts

Keyboard shortcuts are the secret weapon of professional editors. Learning the most commonly used shortcuts can significantly speed up your workflow and make editing feel more intuitive.

  • Cut: Cmd/Ctrl + K
  • Ripple Delete: Shift + Delete
  • Zoom In/Out: + / -
  • Next/Previous Frame: Arrow keys
  • Render: Enter (Premiere Pro) / Cmd+Shift+R (DaVinci)
💡 Pro Tip: Customize your shortcuts to match your workflow. Most editing software allows you to create custom keyboard layouts that fit your editing style perfectly.

2. Organize Your Project Files

A well-organized project is the foundation of efficient editing. Create a clear folder structure on your computer and within your editing software.

  • Project Folder: Main project folder with project name
  • Footage: All raw video files
  • Audio: Music, sound effects, voiceovers
  • Assets: Graphics, templates, overlays
  • Exports: Final rendered videos

3. Learn Color Grading Basics

Color grading can transform your video from amateur to professional. Start with basic color correction before moving to creative grading.

  • Adjust white balance first
  • Use scopes to monitor your levels
  • Apply a LUT (Look Up Table) as a starting point
  • Always color grade in a color-accurate environment

4. Use J and L Cuts

J-cuts (video cuts before audio) and L-cuts (audio cuts before video) create smoother transitions between scenes. This technique makes your edits feel more natural and professional.

5. Master Audio Levels

Bad audio is one of the most common reasons viewers stop watching. Learn to mix audio properly and use compression and EQ to enhance clarity.

  • Dialogue should be around -12dB to -6dB
  • Music should be -20dB to -15dB
  • Sound effects at -15dB to -10dB

6. Learn from Others

Watch tutorials, analyze other editors' work, and never stop learning. The video editing community is incredibly supportive and there's always something new to discover.

7. Don't Overuse Transitions

While transitions can be fun, overusing them can make your video feel amateurish. Simple cuts are often more effective than flashy transitions.

💡 Pro Tip: Use transitions with purpose. Each transition should serve the story you're telling, not just look cool.

8. Tell a Story

Every video should tell a story. Think about the narrative structure and emotional journey you want your audience to experience. Your editing choices should support that story.

9. Export Settings Matter

Knowing the right export settings for your platform is crucial. YouTube, Vimeo, Instagram - each platform has specific requirements for the best quality.

  • YouTube: H.264, 1080p/4K, 25-30 Mbps
  • Instagram: H.264, 1080x1080, 10-15 Mbps
  • Vimeo: H.264, 4K, 50-60 Mbps

10. Practice Consistently

The most important tip - practice consistently. Set aside time each week to edit. Challenge yourself with new projects. The more you edit, the better you'll become.

Final Thoughts

Improving your video editing skills is a journey, not a destination. These 10 tips are just the beginning. Keep learning, keep experimenting, and most importantly, keep creating.
